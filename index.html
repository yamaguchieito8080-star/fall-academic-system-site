Fall Academic System

Fall Academic System is a personal study planner built and used by one student, Eito Yamaguchi. It tracks assignments, deadlines and study sessions, and it can mirror deadlines to the owner's own Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

It is not a public product. It is not offered to other people, and nobody else is asked to sign in to it.
